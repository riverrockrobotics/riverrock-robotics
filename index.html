Product Line 03 · Tool & FOD Accountability

TOOLTRACK
INTELLIGENCE

The lost-tool problem costs American manufacturers millions annually in rework, downtime, and FOD incidents. RFID systems solve it — but at capital costs that kill the ROI calculation. ToolTrack solves it differently.

Request Site Assessment Compare vs RFID
THE PROBLEM · COST OF LOST TOOLS
FOD Events in Aerospace & Precision Mfg
A single Foreign Object Damage event can halt an entire production line. A dropped tool in a high-tolerance environment can cause six-figure rework.
Contaminated Environments
Food processing, pharmaceutical, and cleanroom environments require certified tool hygiene verification at every shift boundary. Manual sign-out sheets fail consistently.
The RFID Cost Barrier
Full RFID tool tracking systems require tagging every individual item, specialized readers, and infrastructure investments that are cost-prohibitive for most shops. ToolTrack delivers equivalent accountability without the capital outlay.
THE SOLUTION · TOOLTRACK INTELLIGENCE
Vision-Verified Check-Out / Check-In
The cart or crib terminal photographs and logs every tool removed and returned. No RFID tags required. The system knows what left and what came back — automatically.
Hygiene Environment Enforcement
Shift-boundary tool reconciliation with supervisor alert if an item is outstanding at end-of-shift. Every tool accounted for before the next crew touches the environment.
FOD Prevention Protocol
Automatic count reconciliation at shift end or job completion. System flags any discrepancy before work continues. Creates a defensible, auditable FOD record with timestamps and operator attribution.
Snap-Mount on Any Cart or Crib
Same magnetic-mount architecture as the cabinet line. Retrofits to existing tool carts, shadow boards, and cribs in minutes. No new furniture required.
